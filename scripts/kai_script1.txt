[CORPORATE BOSS - OFFICE ENTRANCE]

Early morning. Soft golden light streams through the glass facade of a modern high-rise. Kai walks into frame from a distance, charcoal grey tailored suit fitting perfectly, white button-down crisp, a leather briefcase in hand.

He approaches the revolving doors of the corporate tower. Confident stride. Composed expression. A smartwatch glints on his wrist as he adjusts his tie. The camera follows him through the lobby — marble floors, minimalist architecture, soft ambient lighting.

He nods at the receptionist, swipes his access card, and walks toward the elevator. Just before the doors close, he turns to camera with a subtle, knowing smile. The elevator doors slide shut.

Fade to: A desk setup — dual monitors, a steaming flat white, natural light spilling across a leather notebook.
